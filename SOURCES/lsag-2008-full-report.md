## What this closes, and what it doesn't
Closes blackhole-002's central open question: whether LSAG's full report treats the same-theory argument and the astrophysical check as one argument or two. It states this directly — see [[blackhole-003]].

Does not fully close blackhole-001's open question (collision-geometry equivalence between fixed-target cosmic-ray impacts and the LHC's head-on center-of-mass collisions). The full report acknowledges this exact gap exists — "there is one significant difference between cosmic-ray collisions with a body at rest and collisions at the LHC, namely that any massive new particles produced by the LHC collisions will tend to have low velocities, whereas cosmic-ray collisions would produce them with high velocities" — but defers the detailed resolution to a separate paper (Giddings & Mangano, arXiv:0806.3381, cited as reference [2] throughout), not ingested here. A real, named gap, one level deeper than before rather than fully closed.
