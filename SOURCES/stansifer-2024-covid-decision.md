# Stansifer 2024 — Independent judge's full written decision

One of two judges in the Rootclaim $100,000 COVID-origins debate. Per the debate's own rules, both judges decided independently, without conferring. Stansifer, an applied mathematician (PhD, MIT, background in mathematical virology), built his own independent Bayesian location-distance model rather than adopting either debater's framework, and found zoonotic spillover more likely by a Bayes factor of 100 to 1000 under his central model (narrowing to 10000 under one restricted framing he also names).

The other judge, Will van Treuren, judged independently and reached the same zoonotic conclusion through two required-to-converge methods of his own (a holistic synthesis and a Bayesian calculation), with his decisive factors centered on WIV's capability/prior odds and the Huanan Seafood Market epicenter question — not, as this file previously and inaccurately stated, "weighting the biology more heavily." That earlier line came from a secondhand impression rather than his written decision and got corrected once his actual document entered the vault. **Update, 2026-07-17:** Van Treuren's PDF and linked Bayesian-model spreadsheet, previously blocked by the hosting format described below, now sit in the vault directly — see [[van-treuren-2024-covid-decision]].
