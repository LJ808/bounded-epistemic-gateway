# Van Treuren 2024 — Independent judge's full written decision

The second of two judges in the Rootclaim $100,000 COVID-origins debate. Per the debate's own rules, both judges decided independently, without conferring. Van Treuren, a PhD microbiologist and bioinformatician (Stanford), used two independent methods that he required to reach the same conclusion before finalizing his verdict: a holistic synthesis of the strongest case for each side (Method 1), and a Bayesian calculation modeled on the framework the lab-leak side itself preferred (Method 2). Both methods concluded zoonotic spillover more likely — a combined final ratio of P(lab leak)/P(zoonotic) = 3.6×10⁻³, roughly a 1-in-300 chance of lab leak.

This document previously sat behind a Google Drive viewer requiring interactive JavaScript rendering, not fetchable with this vault's tools — see the note in `stansifer-2024-covid-decision.md` and `crux-analysis-v1.md` describing it as blocked. Jay provided the PDF and its linked Bayesian-model spreadsheet directly this session, closing that gap.

**Correction to a prior secondhand description in this vault:** `stansifer-2024-covid-decision.md` previously stated Van Treuren reached his conclusion "weighting the biology more heavily" than Stansifer, sourced to "his own decision-announcement video" rather than his written decision. Reading the actual document finds this inaccurate. Van Treuren's own text names his decisive factors as the prior probability that WIV could have carried out DEFUSE-style research and possessed a suitable genetic backbone, and the probability that the Huanan Seafood Market specifically represented the outbreak's first superspreader location — not a biology-weighting distinction from Stansifer's approach.
